### EPA AE1 - Assessor Pack

This video explains the contents of the EPA assessor pack used for the Digital and Technology Solutions Degree Apprenticeship. It outlines the purpose of each document, including the feedback sheet, question bank, overview guide, and grading spreadsheet. The video is important because it helps assessors understand how to record their questions, evaluate apprentice performance, and apply consistent grading for the work‑based project assessment.
