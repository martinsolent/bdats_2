# Level 6 (3rd year) Modules

![ Banner](/../bdats_2/docs/images/level_banner/L6_banner.png)

The third year, or Level 6, is the final stage of the degree and involves study at an advanced level. Apprentices are expected to demonstrate a high level of independence, critical thinking, and subject expertise. Work at this stage is more complex and often includes larger projects or specialised modules linked to their role.

At Level 6, apprentices will also be supported in preparing for their End Point Assessment. This includes guidance on the required components, such as portfolio items, projects, or professional discussions, ensuring they are fully ready to complete their apprenticeship and achieve their qualification.
