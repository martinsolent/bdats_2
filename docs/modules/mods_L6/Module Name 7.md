# End Point Assessment COM628

This module represents the final stage of both the Degree Apprenticeship and the apprenticeship programme. Apprentices will complete and finalise their 6,000-word work-based project report, which forms part of End-Point Assessment (EPA) Element 1.

Throughout the module, apprentices will receive support and guidance to help them complete their report, prepare for their presentation, and develop their approach to the associated question-and-answer session. This support will align with the requirements and assessment guidelines set out within the Digital and Technology Solutions Professional Degree Apprenticeship Standard.

Apprentices will have already submitted their Portfolio of Evidence (PoE) at Gateway. The module will also provide support and guidance to help apprentices prepare for and undertake the Professional Discussion that underpins this assessment element, enabling them to demonstrate the knowledge, skills and behaviours expected of a competent digital and technology professional.
