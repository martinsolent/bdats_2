# Gateway (Zero Credit Module EPA Prep) COM627

 Apprentices should have collected a range of potential work-based portfolio items throughout their apprenticeship. They will now select six of their best pieces and document these using the provided template, mapping them to the KSBs for assessment element 2. This will support their professional discussion, which is part of assessment element 2 and takes place during the EPA.
 
In the final module, COM627, apprentices will focus on developing their portfolio during study days. They will receive tutor support and feedback throughout, up to gateway submission. At gateway, they will submit their portfolio along with an up-to-date off the job tracker and a module KSB mapping matrix.
