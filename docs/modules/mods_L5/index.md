# Level 5 (2nd year) Modules

![ Banner](/../bdats_2/docs/images/level_banner/L5_banner.png)

The second year, or Level 5, builds on the knowledge gained in the first year and moves learning to a higher level. This is where grades begin to count towards the final degree classification. Apprentices are expected to apply their knowledge more independently, develop stronger analytical skills, and link their learning more closely to their role in the workplace.

At Level 5, apprentices will begin collecting potential work-based portfolio evidence for their End Point Assessment, and they will receive ongoing support for this throughout both second and third year leading up to the assessment itself.
