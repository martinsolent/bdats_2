# Introduction to Problem Solving Through Programming (COM411)

This module introduces you to the fundamental principles of computational thinking, problem solving and software development. Throughout the module, you will learn how to analyse problems, design appropriate solutions and implement those solutions using the Python programming language. The skills you develop will form an important foundation for further study in computing and for future professional practice within the technology sector.

The module is designed around a practical, hands-on approach to learning. You will be encouraged to think critically, explore different ways of solving problems and apply structured techniques to develop working software solutions. As your knowledge grows, you will learn how to design, implement, test and debug programs while developing an understanding of the wider professional, ethical and legal responsibilities associated with software development.

A significant focus of the module is the development of professional skills. You will be introduced to industry-standard tools and practices that are commonly used by software developers. This includes working with Integrated Development Environments (IDEs), particularly PyCharm, and learning how to use version control tools such as Git and GitHub to manage changes to your code and maintain a professional development workflow. These tools will support your learning throughout the module and help prepare you for industry practice.

As part of your studies, you will develop an understanding of programming fundamentals, including input and output, data types, variables and operators. These concepts will provide the building blocks for developing software solutions and will enable you to create programs that process information, make decisions and solve computational problems.

The module also encourages you to engage with the wider computing community. You will be encouraged to develop professional networking skills, participate in relevant industry activities and begin building a professional profile that demonstrates your developing knowledge and abilities. Alongside technical skills, you will explore professional standards, coding conventions and the legal and ethical considerations that influence modern software development practice.

Assessment within the module is designed to reflect authentic computing practice. You will demonstrate your understanding through a time-constrained assessment and through the development of a software product supported by a written report. These assessments will allow you to apply the knowledge and skills gained throughout the module to realistic programming challenges and demonstrate your ability to develop effective software solutions.

By the end of the module, you will have developed a solid understanding of structured problem solving, gained experience in software development using Python and acquired valuable professional skills that will support your progression throughout your degree and into the computing profession.

#### You will develop knowledge and practical skills in the following technical areas:

- Designing solutions before implementation.

- Applying analytical and critical thinking techniques to computing problems.
